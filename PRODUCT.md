## Platform

web

## Users

Immigrants and international students settling in Canada — newcomers navigating career transition, community-building, and the practical unwritten realities of life in a new country (housing, banking, credentials, workplace culture).

## Product Purpose

Migrant Smart helps immigrants and international students land, settle, and thrive in Canada. It exists to replace guesswork with guidance and isolation with belonging, bundling career coaching, a peer community, live events, and practical education into one place so newcomers don't have to piece together their transition from scattered, generic resources.

## Positioning

The all-in-one bundle is the differentiator: career coaching (Career Clinic), a peer community (Mastermind), live events (Community Events), and education (Webinars & Workshops) are stronger together than any single piece would be alone against competing settlement agencies, immigrant career blogs/YouTube channels, or generic networking groups. No single feature is positioned as uniquely defensible in isolation — the combination and continuity across a newcomer's first months in Canada is the product.

## Operating Context

- **Career Clinic** — 1:1 coaching ($75/hour), booked via Calendly, paid via Stripe. Also a structured 6-week program: Assess & localize → Position yourself → Job-search strategy → Land & negotiate.
- **Mastermind Community** — WhatsApp-based peer community (not an in-house forum/app), weekly check-ins, monthly live call.
- **Community Events** — Next Gen (flagship gathering), MS Sports (seasonal social), Young, Skilled & Smart (podcast), The Keys We Carry (documentary). Registration via Luma.
- **Webinars & Workshops** — Newcomers Orientation (flagship, free) plus topic workshops (credentials recognition, banking/credit, Canadian workplace culture, housing). Registration via Luma.
- **Blog** — articles across Settling In, Careers, Community, and Success Stories categories.
- **Founder** — Abigail Peterson, lead coach, hosts the Career Clinic program personally.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Session booking and payment run through Calendly + Stripe (external, not a custom in-house checkout).
- Community lives on WhatsApp; there is no first-party community platform.
- Event/webinar registration runs through Luma (external).
- Newsletter signup is a form only — no confirmed email-service-provider integration behind it yet.
- No confirmed multi-language/i18n requirement; standard web accessibility practices apply (no additional standard or accommodation specified).

## Product Principles

1. **Bundle over single feature.** The coaching, community, events, and education pieces are marketed and designed as a connected whole — avoid treating or selling any one of them as a fully independent standalone product.
2. **Guidance over guesswork.** Every surface should give newcomers something concrete and actionable (a checklist, a step-by-step program, a specific next action), not just reassurance.
3. **Belonging over isolation.** Community and peer accountability (the Mastermind, events) are core to the product's value, not a secondary nice-to-have bolted onto coaching.
4. **Founder-led credibility, in support of the bundle.** Abigail's personal migrant experience underpins trust in the coaching specifically, but it supports the all-in-one positioning rather than being the product's primary differentiator on its own.
5. **Placeholder discipline.** Never fabricate or extend testimonials, member stats, or photos beyond what's explicitly marked real in this document — real evidence must replace every placeholder before this product truthfully claims social proof.
